# Name and logo

The code of Eksoda is free software under the [GNU General Public License v3.0](LICENSE)
(GPL-3.0-or-later). The license covers the code only. As section 7(e) of the license allows,
**it grants no rights to the name "Eksoda" or to the Eksoda logo and app icon.**

## What you may do

- Use, study, change and share the code under the terms of the GPL, including publishing your
  own app built from it, on Google Play or anywhere else, **as long as you also publish its
  complete source code under the GPL**, keep the copyright notices and say what you changed.
- Say that your app is "based on Eksoda" or "a fork of Eksoda", with a link to this repository.
- Use the name and logo to refer to this project, for example in an article or a review.

## What you may not do

- Publish an app, modified or not, under the name "Eksoda" or a confusingly similar name, or with
  the Eksoda logo or app icon.
- Use the application id `io.eksoda`. A fork needs its own id, name and icon, so that nobody can
  mistake it for the original app or for an update of it.
- Suggest that your app is made, endorsed or supported by the Eksoda author.

Unmodified copies of an official release APK, shared as they are and with a link to this
repository, are fine.

Apps that copy the name or the icon will be reported to the store that publishes them as
impersonation.
